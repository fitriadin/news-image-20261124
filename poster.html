LEAD STORY • DISASTER • BANTEN, INDONESIA

24 dead as factory fire rages in Banten

A massive fire swept through a factory in Indonesia’s Banten province, killing at least 24 people. Rescue teams are still searching the charred building for around 30 workers still missing, as the toll climbs through the day.

■ The blaze broke out in the industrial estate and spread fast through the plant; firefighters battled it for hours while families waited outside for news.

■ It is one of Indonesia’s deadliest industrial fires in years, and the search for the missing continues into the night.

■ Officials have yet to say what started the fire; an investigation is under way.
